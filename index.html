The LIKHA difference

Good chocolate
starts at the
root.

We believe chocolate can be deeply indulgent and deeply considered. So we start with exceptional cacao, use creamy Philippine coconut milk, and let the ingredients speak for themselves.

LIKHA means “to create.” It is a practice of making with intention, honoring the land, the people who grow it, and the small pleasure of a really good bar.

Why plant based ↗

01 / Where it begins

Rooted inthe Philippines.

From the cacao farms of Davao to our kitchen in Manila, every LIKHA bar begins with a fruit grown by hand and cared for through the seasons. We roast and refine each batch with Philippine coconut milk, ube, mango, pili, and the steady patience of the people who make cacao possible.
