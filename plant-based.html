The LIKHA difference

Why plant
based.

Because the Philippines already grows a beautiful language of flavor. We want to listen to it, work with it, and leave more room for it to thrive.

A gentler way to make chocolate

Start with
what is here.

Plant based chocolate is not about taking something away. It is about making space for cacao, coconut, fruit, nuts, and roots that already belong to the places we call home.

Grown close to home

A garden
of flavor.

01

Cacao

The beginning of every LIKHA bar, grown in the warm soil and generous rain of the Philippines.

02

Coconut

Coconut milk brings a soft, creamy texture and a familiar island richness to the chocolate.

03

Ube

Velvety purple yam adds a floral earthiness that feels both nostalgic and new.

04

Bright, fragrant mango brings the sunlight of tropical afternoons into every bite.

05

Pili

Buttery pili nuts add a distinctly Filipino depth and a gentle roasted finish.

06

Calamansi

Small citrus with a lively edge, bringing freshness and lift to rich cacao.

What we give back

Good soil,
good future.

Every ingredient connects the bar to a living landscape. Our responsibility is to keep that connection visible, support the people who care for it, and make choices that respect the pace of nature.

That means local cacao, thoughtful batches, less dependence on animal agriculture, and a business that gives back to the farmers and forests that make this work possible.
